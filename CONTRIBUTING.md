# Contributing to Wusel

Wusel is developed by **IT Beratung Hermann GmbH (ITBH)** and is open under
Apache-2.0. Contributions are welcome — on a simple, honest basis. We would
genuinely like to make Wusel better and are glad for help; we just will not let
the project be buried in noise. If it heads there, we go back to developing it
internally.

For full transparency: Wusel itself is built **with AI assistance** — not as a
shortcut. Humans still do the thinking: the design is specified, reviewed,
tested, revised and re-validated by people; the AI assists that work, it does not
replace the judgement or the testing. That is exactly the bar we ask of any
contribution.

## Code

- **AI-assisted code is as welcome as human-written code.** We do not care how a
  change was produced — only whether it is correct, carefully **tested**, and
  fits. The bar is the same for everyone.
- **It has to be yours to give.** The code must be legally clean too — your own
  or compatibly licensed, offered under Apache-2.0. Code lifted from a
  proprietary or licence-incompatible source is not acceptable however well it
  works, whether a person or a machine produced it. By contributing you confirm
  you have the right to.
- **ITBH decides what to review and integrate.** We may leave a contribution
  unmerged or not act on it at all; there is no obligation to merge, or even
  respond to, everything.
- **Not to your taste? Fork it.** That is what the open licence is for — good
  luck, and have fun with it.

## Issues & bug reports

- **Reports are very welcome**, and using AI to help write a clear one is fine.
- **A human must stand behind every report** — someone who understands what it is
  about and can engage about it.
- **Low-effort noise is ignored**: pseudo-findings, duplicate pile-ons, automated
  mass submissions, or a stream of unreviewed AI-generated reports, from that
  source onward.

**In short:** behave decently and genuinely want to help move Wusel forward, and
you are welcome here.

The full version is the **Contributing** page in the documentation
(`documentation/modules/ROOT/pages/contributing.adoc`).
